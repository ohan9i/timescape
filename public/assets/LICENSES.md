# Forest Window asset sources and permissions

Forest Window is the application's sole background scene. It uses actual 3D plant geometry and scanned surface materials, with lighting, seasons, wind and weather generated by the application. No static landscape photograph or seasonal background replacement is used.

All third-party models and scanned textures shipped in `assets/` are from Poly Haven under CC0 1.0 (public domain), permitting redistribution, adaptation and commercial use.

- **Island Tree 02** — https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02 — optimized near/far tree geometry and 1K trunk, leaf and branch materials in `forest/island_tree_02/`. Authors: Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers.
- **Searsia Lucida** — https://polyhaven.com/a/searsia_lucida — four losslessly packed plant variants and 1K texture atlas in `forest/searsia_lucida/`. Authors: James Ray Cock and Jenelle van Heerden.
- **Fern 02** — https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02 — four 3D fern variants and 1K materials in `forest/fern_02/`. Authors: Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers.
- **Wood Table** — https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table — `forest/wood-color.jpg`, `forest/wood-normal.jpg`, `forest/wood-roughness.jpg`. Author: Dimitrios Savva.
- **Forest Ground 04** — https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04 — `forest/ground-color.jpg`, `forest/ground-normal.jpg`. Authors: Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers.
- **Rocky Terrain 02** — https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02 — `rock-color.jpg`, `rock-normal.jpg` (1K), used on Forest Window rocks.

Official license: https://polyhaven.com/license . CC0 legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .

Full author credits, exact original download URLs and hashes, mesh optimization details, and the distinction between deployed files and unused research downloads are recorded in [Forest Window asset provenance](./FOREST_ASSET_SOURCES.md) and `forest/source-manifest.json`. Manifest hashes refer to original downloads; optimized runtime glTF/BIN files have different hashes.

The scene is an artistic composition, not a botanically or geographically exact reconstruction. Legacy Mountain Lake elevation data, pine atlases and unused rock ARM material are no longer distributed with the application.

- **Forest Leaves 02** — https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_leaves_02 — Rob Tuytel. CC0 scanned organic forest ground: 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, ARM and displacement maps at `forest/high/ground-*.jpg`. Official file sizes and MD5 validated; local SHA-256 and exact download URLs in `forest/high/ground-source-manifest.json`.
